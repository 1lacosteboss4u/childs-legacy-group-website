Funding standard

Every proposal should earn capital.

The same underwriting framework should apply to every family applicant so decisions stay fair, documented, and defensible.

Business Plan

Problem, customer, competitive advantage, operating model, and what the company must prove.

Owner Contribution

Money, assets, time, guarantees or other commitment the founder contributes.

Revenue & Market

Pricing, margins, customer acquisition, competition, demand and realistic market size.

Funding Structure

Debt, equity, revenue share or another structure with clear rights, obligations and exit terms.

Management

Who operates the company, experience, key-person risk and capability gaps.

Risk

What can fail, who absorbs the downside, and what controls can reduce damage.

Profit Plan

How the venture creates sustainable value, cash flow, distributions or reinvestment.

Exit Strategy

How the family's capital eventually returns, refinances, sells, converts or exits.

Conflict protection matters. Related parties should be disclosed, conflicted decision-makers should recuse themselves, and funding should be documented through professionally reviewed agreements before capital is committed.

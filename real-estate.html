Acquisition discipline

Buy the numbers, not the excitement.

Every deal should be tested against financing, vacancy, insurance, taxes, maintenance, capital expenses, reserves, debt service, cash flow, equity and exit strategy.

Acquisition

Purchase price, closing costs, renovation budget, inspections and total cash required.

Financing

Down payment, interest rate, debt service, term, lender requirements and refinance risk.

Operations

Rent, vacancy, taxes, insurance, utilities, management, repairs and capital expenses.

Exit

Hold period, sale assumptions, refinance options, liquidity and tax considerations.

Stress test every deal. Model vacancy, major repairs, higher insurance/taxes, weak rent growth and unfavorable refinancing before recommending a purchase.

Safety-first system

Confidence without pretending every repair is DIY-safe.

Electrical paths emphasize power-off verification. Roofing paths stop for unsafe height or weather conditions. HVAC stops before refrigerant, gas, combustion, or major electrical work. The product is designed to tell users when to stop and call a qualified professional.
